| **CR-24** ✅ | **ΔT measurement method locked** (M2-1c). ΔT = `T_hotspot − T_baseline` from the true °C array. `T_hotspot` is the **P99** temperature of the proposed hotspot region — a robust high percentile that rejects the single-pixel sensor-noise spike `max` would catch, yet stays near the hotspot's true peak (P95 under-reports a compact hotspot). `T_baseline` is the **median** of a local baseline ring — the proposal box grown by 1.0× its size on each side, EXCLUDING the hotspot; the median rejects field outliers. Clamped at 0, rounded to 0.1 °C, `metric_unit = CELSIUS_DELTA`. A quiet (uniform) radiometric frame → ΔT ≈ 0, a VALID low/no-anomaly outcome — distinct from the M2-1b fail-closed (which is only for NO radiometric channel). Monotonicity (CR-12): a higher ΔT can only ESCALATE via `served_priority = max(class_category, ΔT_category)`, never downgrade. v1 uses object-signal temperature at default emissivity; atmospheric/emissivity correction is a later refinement. | ΔT is the client-facing measurement — it must be robust, deterministic, and monotonic, not 'a number'. Single-pixel max and whole-frame mean are both noise/gradient-sensitive; a high-percentile hotspot vs. a local-median baseline is the defensible choice. The IEC category *thresholds* the ΔT feeds stay provisional — validating them against IEC TS 62446-3 is M2-2 (CR-19 closure), NOT retuned here. |
